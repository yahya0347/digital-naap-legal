Delete Your Digital Naap Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Digital Naap account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to support.digitalnaap@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Digital Naap account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include your registered email address / account username in the message.

Once we receive your request, your account will be deleted within 30 days.

What data is deleted

The following data associated with your account will be permanently deleted:

  • Your profile information (name, email address)
  • Customer records you created in the app
  • Order and calendar/earnings history
  • Any saved settings and preferences

What data may be retained

We may retain limited transaction records (such as subscription purchase records) where required for legal, tax, or accounting purposes, for up to the period required by applicable law.

Contact

For any questions about this process, contact us at support.digitalnaap@gmail.com.
