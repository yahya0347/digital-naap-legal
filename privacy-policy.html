Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Digital Naap ("Digital Naap," "we," "us," or "our") provides a mobile application that helps tailors and small tailoring businesses manage their customers, measurements, orders, and payments (the "App" or "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Note on how this app is used: Digital Naap is a business tool ("B2B"). The person who creates an account (the "Account Holder") is typically a tailor or tailoring business, and the App is used to record information about that Account Holder's own customers. Digital Naap is not a consumer-facing social app, and it is not directed at the general public or at children.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

When you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service); we do not store your password ourselves — Firebase stores it in encrypted form on our behalf.

1.2 Business data you enter about your own customers

As part of using the App, Account Holders enter information about their own customers in order to run their tailoring business. This may include:

  • Customer name, phone number, and optional notes
  • Body measurements (e.g. chest, waist, shoulder, sleeve length)
  • Order details (description, status, total amount, order date)
  • Payment records (amounts paid and payment dates)

This information is provided and controlled by the Account Holder, not by us. The Account Holder is responsible for having the appropriate permission from their own customers to record this information and for complying with any privacy or data protection laws that apply to their business. Digital Naap acts as a service provider that stores and syncs this data on the Account Holder's behalf.

1.3 Advertising identifier (free tier only)

If you use the free, ad-supported tier of the App, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may use your device's advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads, subject to your device's ad tracking permission settings. See Section 3.2 below for details. This identifier is used only for ad delivery and is never linked to your customer or business data described in Section 1.2.

2. Where and How Your Data Is Stored

Data you enter is stored in two places:

  • On your device, in a local database (SQLite), so the App works even without an internet connection.
  • In the cloud, as a backup and to sync your data across devices, using Firebase Cloud Firestore (a Google Cloud service). Cloud data is stored under your individual account, keyed to your unique user ID, so that one Account Holder's data is kept separate from another's.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including relying on Firebase's built-in access controls that restrict cloud-stored data to the account that created it. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

Service Purpose
Firebase Authentication (Google) Creating your account and signing you in securely.
Firebase Cloud Firestore (Google) Cloud backup and sync of your business data across devices.
Google AdMob Displaying ads to free-tier users (see 3.2).
Apple App Store / Google Play Billing Processing Pro subscription payments (see 3.3).

3.1 Firebase (Google)

We use Firebase for authentication and for syncing your data to the cloud so it survives an app reinstall or shows up on a second device. Firebase is operated by Google and data is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. You can learn more about how Google handles data through Firebase at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3.2 Google AdMob (free tier only)

If you use the free tier, the App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to select and measure ads, and — on iOS — this requires your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ad-related identifiers are handled separately from, and are never combined with, the customer, order, measurement, or payment data described in Section 1.2. Pro tier subscribers do not see ads and are not subject to ad tracking. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3.3 Apple App Store / Google Play Billing (Pro tier only)

Pro tier subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google's Play Store billing systems, depending on your device. We do not collect, see, or store your payment card details or other billing information — that is handled directly between you and Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active, so the App remains useful to you. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, please contact us using the email address below and we will delete it within a reasonable time.

5. Children's Privacy

Digital Naap is a business tool intended for use by adults operating or working at a tailoring business. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Your Choices

  • You can review and edit most of the business data you've entered directly within the App.
  • You can switch to the Pro tier to stop seeing ads and remove the use of the advertising identifier described in Section 3.2.
  • On iOS, you can control ad tracking permission at any time via Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data as described in Section 4.
